## Fixes, ranked by seats hurt × severity

1. **League pages still read as a draft tool in Week 12.** League pages open with "AFTER-ACTION REPORT READY. Your draft is complete... 90-day playbook", a Value vs ADP checkpoint, "3 OF 4 PICKS IN", "RB run on. 4 of last 8 picks were RBs", and "DOCTRINE DRIFT DETECTED. Your last 8 picks read as future-leaning". Hurts: All 11. Owner: Rethink the app for in-season play (PR #142 moves part of this; re-check after merge).
2. **Redraft and best ball pages talk about 2028.** 777. (redraft): "Peak projects 2026... Future capital sold off... 5 future R1s". Smells like jabroni spirit (best ball): "Patient build. War year projects 2028" and an "ATTACK A TRADE" button. Build fit compares every league to "82 real dynasty teams". Hurts: Tyler, Ray, Marcus, Isaiah. Owner: Rethink the app for in-season play.
3. **The same team gets two identities.** 12 Man. at 7-4 and 70% playoffs: /home says "Lean sell", the league page says "REBUILDER... Patient build". lincolnenglish, 0-11 and tanking: "Mid-pack. Two playable lenses". ladovina11, 9-2 and first: "We are in the hunt", while /home's ranges call 80%+ a Favorite. Best ball record reads "11-11" on the league page and "5-6" on /home. Sent to the stats steward to check, not asserted as wrong. Hurts: Jordan, Priya, Vic, Ray. Owner: Rethink the app for in-season play, with a stats-steward check on the posture read.
4. **Winners never get a buy move.** Every /home call is sell, keep or nothing: "Keep your roster: no sale beats holding it" for the 9-2 team. League shifts already tags "3 are now sellers" but nothing turns that into "buy X from Y, title chance +N". Hurts: Marcus, Dana, Priya. Owner: Design the multi-league home page and the weekly email (shared decision items), with trade-advisor.
5. **Coach is still the draft Coach.** Quick prompts: "Compare my next-pick options", "Should I trade up from my next slot?" in Week 12. "Talk it through with Coach" from the Warren card opens an empty chat that does not carry the trade. Opening Coach without ?username= dead-ends: "No username on the URL", even when signed in. Hurts: All 11. Owner: Rethink the app for in-season play (Coach page), with Coach quality.
6. **Losing teams get nothing to look forward to.** <2% leagues read "Nothing to trade this week" or "no sale is worth chasing yet". No projected draft slot for Sam's seven 1sts, no spoiler or rivalry hook for Kelsey, no injured-contender scan for Vic. Hurts: Kelsey, Sam, Vic. Owner: Design the multi-league home page and the weekly email.
7. **Floating pills cover the trade card on phones.** At phone width the "WHAT'S NEW" and "FEEDBACK" pills sit on top of the Europa trade card, the first decision on the page. Hurts: Dana, Tyler, Kelsey, Marcus. Owner: Design the multi-league home page (site-wide chrome).
8. **Value scale and 'fair share' are jargon for casual players.** "about 24 value points on our 0 to 100 scale", "Win the league <2%, fair share is 10%". The same line "The analysts lean sell, but no sale is worth chasing yet" repeats on two cards. Hurts: Tyler, Dana, Kelsey. Owner: Weekly email brief (owns decision-item wording for /home too).
9. **No 'this week' for in-season fun players.** /home has no matchup, opponent, or swing game for this week; the Sunday Game Day email is not built yet. Fun seats scored Fun 1-2 everywhere. Hurts: Dana, Tyler, Kelsey, Ray. Owner: Weekly email brief (Sunday Game Day), then the multi-league home page.
10. **The homepage speaks to sellers and analysts.** The only sample is a sell ("Offer your 32-year-old WR1"). Nothing shows a contender buying, a casual redraft player, or a losing team's reason to stay. Hurts: Marcus, Dana, Kelsey, Tyler. Owner: Marketing site.
